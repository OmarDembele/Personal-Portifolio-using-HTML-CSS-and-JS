My Journey

A little about my journey

I am originally from Senegal, a country that is part of my identity and has shaped who I am today. My roots, experiences, and values have taught me the importance of hard work, perseverance, and staying humble.

My journey has been filled with learning experiences, challenges, and opportunities. Along the way, I developed an interest in technology and discovered my passion for software development.

Moving to the United States opened a new chapter in my life. It gave me the opportunity to continue my education, improve my English, discover new perspectives, and challenge myself in ways I had never imagined.

Today, I am studying at Oakland Community College and continuing to build my skills in technology. I am focused on growing, learning, and creating a better future for myself.

This is only the beginning of my journey.
